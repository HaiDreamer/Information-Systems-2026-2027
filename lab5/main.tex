\noindent
\textbf{\large Which normal form does the employee database satisfy? Why?}

The employee database satisfies 3NF (Third Normal Form).

% ---------------------------------------------------------
% 1NF
% ---------------------------------------------------------

\section{1NF (First Normal Form)}

The requirements of 1NF are:

\begin{itemize}
    \setlength{\itemsep}{3pt}
    \item Each column contains atomic (single) values.
    \item There are no repeating groups.
    \item Each row can be uniquely identified.
\end{itemize}

In this database, all six tables (\texttt{employees},
\texttt{departments}, \texttt{dept\_emp},
\texttt{dept\_manager}, \texttt{salaries}, and
\texttt{titles}) contain atomic values. Each table has a
primary key to uniquely identify its records.

% ---------------------------------------------------------
% 2NF
% ---------------------------------------------------------

\section{2NF (Second Normal Form)}

The requirements of 2NF are:

\begin{itemize}
    \setlength{\itemsep}{3pt}
    \item Satisfies 1NF.
    \item Every non-key attribute depends on the entire
    primary key (no partial dependency).
\end{itemize}

Tables with a single-column primary key
(\texttt{employees}, \texttt{departments}) automatically
satisfy 2NF.

Tables with composite primary keys
(\texttt{dept\_emp}, \texttt{dept\_manager},
\texttt{salaries}, \texttt{titles}) have non-key attributes
that depend on the entire composite key.

% ---------------------------------------------------------
% 3NF
% ---------------------------------------------------------

\section{3NF (Third Normal Form)}

The requirements of 3NF are:

\begin{itemize}
    \setlength{\itemsep}{3pt}
    \item Satisfies 2NF.
    \item There are no transitive dependencies (non-key
    attributes must not depend on other non-key attributes).
\end{itemize}

\noindent
\textbf{Check each table:}

\vspace{3pt}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabularx}{0.95\textwidth}{|l|X|}

    \textbf{Table} & \textbf{Functional Dependency} \\
    \hline

    employees &
    $emp\_no \rightarrow birth\_date, first\_name,$
    \newline
    $last\_name, gender, hire\_date$ \\
    \hline

    departments &
    $dept\_no \rightarrow dept\_name$ \\
    \hline

    dept\_emp &
    $(emp\_no, dept\_no) \rightarrow from\_date, to\_date$ \\
    \hline

    dept\_manager &
    $(emp\_no, dept\_no) \rightarrow from\_date, to\_date$ \\
    \hline

    salaries &
    $(emp\_no, from\_date) \rightarrow salary, to\_date$ \\
    \hline

    titles &
    $(emp\_no, title, from\_date) \rightarrow to\_date$ \\
    \hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

In each table, non-key attributes depend directly on the
primary key. There are no transitive dependencies among
non-key attributes.
